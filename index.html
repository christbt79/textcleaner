How Textcleaner works

Paste text and it is cleaned straight away. Then copy it and paste it anywhere. That is the whole job.

Always fixed

Invisible characters, non-breaking spaces, garbled characters such as an apostrophe that arrives as three strange symbols, joined-up letters from PDFs, curly quotes, double spaces, stray spaces at line ends and extra blank lines. The line under the box says exactly what was fixed, and Show original highlights where each one was.

Your choice

  • Join broken lines: for PDFs and emails, where lines break mid-sentence. Also removes page numbers, repeated headers and > reply markers.
  • Remove bullets and numbering from the start of lines.
  • Keep curly quotes and dashes, for InDesign or Word.
  • One line: everything on a single line, for a form field or spreadsheet cell.

If the app spots something one of these would fix, it suggests it.

More tools

Change case, sort lines, remove duplicates, find and replace, strip HTML and reduce to plain ASCII. Each applies once, and Undo takes it back.

Your text stays here

Everything runs in this browser tab. There is no server, no account and no tracking, and the page works with no connection at all.
